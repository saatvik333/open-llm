05 / Questions

Before you start.

Who is it for?

Beginners who want the whole picture, engineers moving into AI, students, and practitioners who want their foundations and the 2024–2026 developments in one place.

Do I need mathematics or AI experience?

No. Every equation is explained in words before symbols, and each part builds on the one before it.

How current is it?

The technology snapshot is {{SNAPSHOT}}. Concepts are meant to last; product names, laws, and capabilities are dated and will change. New releases appear on the releases page.

What may I do with it?

The text uses CC BY 4.0: share and adapt it for any purpose, including commercially, with attribution. Code uses the MIT License. Full terms are in the license file.

How do I verify a download?

Download SHA256SUMS next to the file and run sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing.

Open an issue on the tracker with the section and what you expected. The accessibility notes describe what each format provides.
