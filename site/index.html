QUESTIONS

Before you start

Who is this book for?

Self-directed beginners, software engineers, students, educators, technical leaders, and experienced practitioners rebuilding their foundations.

Do I need previous mathematics or AI experience?

No. Equations are explained in words before symbols, and the book builds its vocabulary progressively.

Was this book written by AI?

Yes. The text was written by AI language models under the direction of the Open LLM Project. Separate AI fact-checking passes then re-opened the cited primary source for each claim, recomputed every number, and ran every code example; the method is described in VERIFICATION.md. Automated checking reduces errors but cannot guarantee their absence, so every claim carries a citation you can follow, and corrections are welcome on the issue tracker.

What may I do with it?

The text and original diagrams use CC BY 4.0: share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, with attribution and a note of any changes. Code samples and build scripts use the MIT License. See the license file for the full terms.
